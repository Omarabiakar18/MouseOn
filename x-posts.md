Key Insights from Research
What goes viral:

Personal underdog story — Student, no VC, building alone hits different
Relatable problem first — Don't lead with product, lead with pain
Specificity — "I lost my cursor 100x a day" > "cursor management tool"
Ask genuinely — "Would love your feedback" beats "please buy"
Short-form + image/video — The 2025 algorithm rewards native media heavily
What flops:

Long threads without killer hooks
Selling before connecting
Generic "check out my app" posts
No visual demo
Draft Posts for You
POST 1: The Psychological Hook (Personal Story + Feedback Ask)
I'm a 23yo dev building my first macOS app

I have a MacBook + monitor + iPad as a second screen

My cursor vanishes 100 times a day. Literally.

So I built MouseOn — shows which display your cursor is on right in the menu bar

Why this works:

Opens with personal identity (creates connection)
Shows the problem with specificity (100 times = relatable)
Doesn't sell — asks for feedback (reciprocity)
Ends with engagement hook (comments boost algo)
